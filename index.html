Hi, I am Aritra. I am a physics graduate student at the University of Colorado Boulder working under the guidance of Prof. Dan Dessau. Prior to my PhD I did my integrated BS-MS from IISER Kolkata with Major in Physics and Minor in Earth Science and Maths.
Currently I am working on instrumentation for a Scanning SQUID(Super Conducting Quantum Interference Devices) setup, which will be used for vortex dynamics study in superconductors along with thermal and magnetic imaging. SQUIDs are very sensitive magnetometers and our SQUIDs will be built on top of very small(100nm) tips which will allow us very high spatial resolution and very low tip sample seperation(~50nm).I have also been fabricating teeny tiny devices (micron sized) from crystals using Focused Ion Beam (FIB) to study novel physics such as the possibility of triplet paired superconductivity and Chirality Induced Spin Selectivity (CISS).
I enjoy designing new experimental setup specially when they work! Other than that I dabble in painting, obsessing over my plants, going into rabit holes about ecological behaviour of animals and plants, get my days ruined by football (Arsenal) and I cook to relax.
